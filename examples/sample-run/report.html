      Methodology
      

      Score = clamp(100 − E − L − R − B − A, 0, 100), rounded to the nearest integer.
Overall is the mean of scored experiments; skips and absent during evidence are excluded.
      

      • E = 40 × min(1, max(0, worst during-minus-baseline error rate) / 0.5).
        • 

      • L = 20 × min(1, max(0, worst during/baseline p95 − 1) / 4).
        • 

      • R = 0 for measured recovery within 10s; 25 if recovery is unproven; otherwise 25 × min(1, recovery seconds / 60).
        • 

      • B = 10 × impacted hypothesis journeys / total baseline journeys (excluding aggregate all).
        • 

      • A = 5 for a metric/blast-radius Watchdog abort; operator KILL/signals do not add this penalty.
      

      Zero baseline p95: zero during p95 has no latency penalty; positive during p95 saturates L.
Sparse scores are descriptive, not hypothesis confirmation. Missing journeys are evidence gaps.
      

      Bands: ≥85 resilient; ≥70 acceptable; ≥50 weak; otherwise fragile.
Severity: critical below 30 or aborted with ≥2 impacted journeys; high below 60; medium below 80; otherwise low.
Confirmed experiments scoring ≥80 appear under What held up, never ranked weaknesses.
      

      Recovery requires three complete healthy HTTP windows for every journey (1–20s, sized from baseline RPS);
container restoration alone cannot prove it. Thresholds and measurement version are recorded
above; deterministic policy clamps remain authoritative. AI explains evidence; deterministic code sets scores.
